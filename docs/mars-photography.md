# Current Mars atmosphere

Active asset: `public/space/mars-curiosity.jpg` (2472 × 1134 JPEG, approximately 664 KB).

Source: [NASA/JPL — Strata at Base of Mount Sharp, PIA19839](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia19839-strata-at-base-of-mount-sharp/)

Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS. Photograph captured by Curiosity's Mastcam on mission sol 580, showing the Kimberley formation. NASA white-balanced this released image to aid geological interpretation; it is not a claim of exact human-eye Martian sky colour.

The original downloaded photograph is preserved without image edits. Responsive cropping, a soft edge mask, and dark readability overlays are applied in the website. No hover motion, generated terrain, or composited rover is used in the current theme.

The old generated dune and rover assets remain on disk for recovery but are no longer referenced by active components. Their historical prompts are in `docs/mars-assets.md`.
